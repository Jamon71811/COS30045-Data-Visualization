Bigger Screen, Heavier Bill

A data-driven storyboard exploring the hidden energy costs of modern televisions.

Phase 1: The Hook

The Hidden Cost

Consumers frequently upgrade to large living room TVs, focusing on 4K resolution while overlooking the steep increase in annual electricity usage and hidden operational costs.

Phase 2: Size Baseline

The Data Context

By examining official energy rating figures, we categorized hundreds of TV models into Small (less than 43"), Medium (44" to 65"), and Large (greater than 66") tiers.

Phase 3: Visual Proof 1

Size vs. Consumption

Power demand scales aggressively: Average consumption jumps from ~128 kWh (Small) to ~380 kWh (Medium) up to ~745 kWh (Large).

Phase 4: Tech Dimension

Panel Technology

Once size is selected, does panel technology matter? We cross-compared LCD, LCD (LED), and OLED display engineering efficiency across the same size tiers.

Phase 5: Visual Proof 2

Tech vs. Size

Technology matters by size: LED draws the most in Large sizes (~760 kWh), while OLED is surprisingly inefficient in Small sizes (~235 kWh).

Phase 6: Action

Smart Shopping

Stick to medium screens for energy parity. If buying a large display, check Star Ratings closely. Avoid small OLEDs purely for energy-saving purposes.
