Panel Technology Comparison

Not all pixels are created equal. Different display technologies require vastly different amounts of electricity to produce the same image brightness. Here is a breakdown of how the three main panel types perform based on our dataset analysis.

Panel Technology Avg. Energy (Small) Avg. Energy (Large) Best Consumer Use Case
Standard LCD ~120 kWh/yr ~650 kWh/yr Budget-friendly, standard viewing environments.
LED (Backlit LCD) ~125 kWh/yr ~760 kWh/yr Bright rooms and vibrant daytime viewing.
OLED ~235 kWh/yr ~715 kWh/yr Dark home theaters, perfect contrast and blacks.
